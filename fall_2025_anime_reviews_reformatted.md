# Very Quick Anime Reviews -- Fall 25

## Rules

- Watch 5 to 10 minutes of each show
- Try to get all the new shows, generally skip continuing/returning seasons
- Ratings: Hard Pass, Pass, Finish Ep, Maybe, Watch, Definitely, Delicious Garbage, Steaming Garbage
