# Very Quick Anime Reviews – Winter 26

## Reviewed (newest reviews at the top) – Completed!

### Dead Account | デッドアカウント

I ended up watching the whole episode; not because it was particularly great or anything, it just was one of those
things where it was there. I was kind of curious about the hook, and it came a little bit later, and then I was already
going, so I completed the episode. The actual show itself is fine. It’s not great, and I’m probably not gonna keep
watching. But it does come from an interesting premise: ghosts have always haunted places that people have strong
attachments to, for example - mirrors, rooms, bathrooms, all these other different places. And that has shifted into the
digital realm and is now focused on mobile phones. So, it’s kind of an interesting modern take on the
ghost-haunting-premise. The actual execution is just meh.  _[Reviewed: 2026-02-04]_

Recommendation: **Finish Ep, since I already did that**

---

### Kaya-chan Isn't Scary | カヤちゃんはコワくない

This show is mid. Its production values are not great. The story could go somewhere fun; I feel like it’s kind of going
for an Anya Forger kind of thing with the main character, but the rest of the show isn’t as Spy Family level. It’s
basically just Anya at preschool. I have no interest in finding out if Kaya-Chan is scary or not.
_[Reviewed: 2026-02-03]_

Recommendation: **Streaming Garbage**

---

### The Daily Life of a Part-time Torturer | 拷問バイトくんの日常

This show has features pretty boys & girls torturing people. The premise is that, apparently, torture is legal in this
world, but it’s very flimsily done. The starting premise is that the main character is supposedly so good at all of his
previous part-time jobs, such that everyone wants him to take over their businesses; but he just he doesn’t want to do
it, he gets bored with the previous 58 jobs he's had. However, he finds this job (literally torturing people) and he
loves it! The _main_ issue is not just that he’s mega-literally torturing people; but, it’s not done in a way where they
actually show him doing the horrible things that he’s doing to people. If you saw him doing those things you would
immediately despise him. Which doesn't work because he’s supposed to be a _likable main character_ even though you see
him sitting there – literally with people strapped down in chairs, then they cut away, the victims making _ouchie_
sounds, and he’s got pliers and other instruments of pain. It’s awful. It's peak 2026+. Don’t watch it.
_[Reviewed: 2026-02-03]_

Recommendation: **Maybe We Shouldn't Make Shows Literally Glorifying Torture**

### Shiboyugi: Playing Death Games to Put Food on the Table | 死亡遊戯で飯を食う。

The first episode is ~50 minutes long, so I watched it for about 15 minutes. I still have no idea about the: characters,
plot, art style, production, ... really anything. It’s supposed to be a death-game-type-thing, but it feels kind of
like a weird version of _Bakemonogatari_ in terms of some of the art style and overlays. But... it’s not done as well,
and I’m not interested in caring about whether these people will make it out alive or not. That sucks because I love me
a good death game and this is just not working for me. There’s no death and there’s no game.
